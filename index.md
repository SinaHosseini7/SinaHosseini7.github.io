I am a chemical engineer working on heat transfer and thermal systems, with both computational and experimental experience. My interest began with videos explaining how heat pipes work.

At the University of Zanjan, I studied aerogel insulation and co-authored a journal article and two conference papers on it. Separately, I wrote Python modules for a porous-media flow simulator. At PANDA, I ran all 16 heat-transfer simulation cases for a battery-powered ladle carrier at Mobarakeh Steel Co. With aerogel insulation and two fans, the simulated average battery temperature stayed below 40 °C beside a ladle wall at about 400 °C. Then, as Head of R&D at Nano Sun Abrad, I redesigned supports and insulation to reduce thermal bridges in cryogenic storage tanks.

Industry was closer to reality than the lab, and it improved my laboratory work. I now start from a fix that has to work in the plant. My MSc at the Chemistry and Chemical Engineering Research Center of Iran, on producing magnesium oxide from magnesite ore, added experimental process work: leaching, managing an experimental budget, and systematic article and patent searches.

Long term, I want to build a company that turns thermal-management research into industrial products. I am interested in PhD opportunities in heat transfer, thermal insulation, and thermal management and happy to discuss possible projects.
